# SET v1.0.2 — Business CRM

Adds a full client billing profile form and a dedicated **My Business** area in the left navigation.

## Client card fields
- First name / last name / role
- Company and legal company name
- Primary email and billing email
- Phone and website
- Country, city, ZIP/postal code, billing address and second address line
- SIREN, SIRET, tax identification number, VAT number
- Optional tax regime
- IBAN, BIC/SWIFT, preferred currency and payment terms
- Internal/billing notes

Client cards remain reusable for invoices and quotes.

## My Business
The dedicated left-navigation page stores issuer identity, company, address, tax, VAT, SIREN/SIRET, banking and invoice-default data. It uses the existing cloud-synced business profile collection and remains available in Finance > Business Profile as well.
